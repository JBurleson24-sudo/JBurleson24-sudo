=====================================
        VIDEO GAME STORE HELP
          Customer Window
=====================================

Browsing Games
--------------
- All available games are displayed in the inventory list.
- Click a game to view its description, price, stock, and image.

Searching
---------
- Enter part of a game's name in the Search box.
- Click the Search button to display matching games.

Filtering by Category
---------------------
- Select a category from the Category drop-down list.
- Choosing "All Categories" displays every game.

Adding Items to the Cart
------------------------
1. Select a game.
2. Choose the desired quantity.
3. Click "Add To Cart".

Note:
You cannot add more copies than are currently in stock.

Viewing Your Cart
-----------------
Click the "View Cart" button to review or purchase your selected items.

Logging In
----------
You may browse the inventory without logging in.
A valid customer login is required before completing checkout.
