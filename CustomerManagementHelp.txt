CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT HELP

The Customer Management screen allows managers to view, search,
add, edit, disable, and enable customer accounts.

SEARCHING FOR CUSTOMERS
-----------------------
1. Enter part or all of a customer's name, username, email,
   or other supported search information in the Search box.
2. Click Search.
3. Matching customers will appear in the customer list.
4. Click Clear to remove the search and display all customers.

ADDING A CUSTOMER
-----------------
1. Click Add Customer.
2. Enter the required customer information.
3. Complete all required account fields.
4. Click Create Account to save the new customer.

EDITING A CUSTOMER
------------------
1. Select a customer from the customer list.
2. Click Edit Customer.
3. Change the desired customer information.
4. Click Save to save the changes.
5. Click Cancel to leave without saving.

DISABLING A CUSTOMER ACCOUNT
----------------------------
1. Select the customer from the customer list.
2. Click Disable / Enable.
3. Confirm the action when prompted.
4. A disabled account is indicated by a checked box in the
   Disabled column.

ENABLING A CUSTOMER ACCOUNT
---------------------------
1. Select a disabled customer from the customer list.
2. Click Disable / Enable.
3. Confirm the action when prompted.
4. The Disabled checkbox will become unchecked.

ACCOUNT STATUS
--------------
Disabled:
The customer account is temporarily disabled. The account
information remains stored in the system.

Deleted:
The Deleted column indicates whether an account has been marked
as deleted.

CLOSE
-----
Click Close to return to the Manager Dashboard.
