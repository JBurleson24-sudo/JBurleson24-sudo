MANAGER REPORTS HELP

SALES REPORTS

Select Date:
Select the date that will be used to generate a sales report.

Daily Sales:
Generates a sales report for the selected date.

Weekly Sales:
Generates a sales report beginning with the selected date and covering seven days.

Monthly Sales:
Generates a sales report for the entire month containing the selected date.


INVENTORY REPORTS

Available Inventory:
Displays inventory items that are currently available for sale.

Items Needing Restock:
Displays inventory items whose quantity on hand is at or below their restock threshold.

All Inventory:
Displays all inventory items.


REPORT INFORMATION

Sales reports display order information including items sold, quantity, price, discounts, tax, and order totals.

Inventory reports display inventory ID, item name, cost, retail price, quantity on hand, restock threshold, and availability.

Reports open in your default web browser.
