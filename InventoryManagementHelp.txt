INVENTORY MANAGEMENT HELP

The Inventory Management screen allows managers to view and maintain
all products stored in inventory.

SEARCHING INVENTORY
- Enter part or all of a product name in the Search box.
- Select a category to limit results to that category.
- Click Search to display matching products.
- Click Clear to remove all search filters and display all inventory.

INVENTORY GRID
The inventory grid displays:
- Product ID
- Product Name
- Cost
- Retail Price
- Quantity on Hand
- Restock Threshold
- Discontinued Status

ADD PRODUCT
1. Click Add Product.
2. Enter the product name and description.
3. Select a category.
4. Enter the product cost and retail price.
5. Enter the starting quantity.
6. Enter the restock threshold.
7. Optionally choose a product image.
8. Click Save.

EDIT PRODUCT
1. Select a product in the inventory grid.
2. Click Edit Product.
3. Update the desired product information.
4. Click Save.

ADJUST QUANTITY
1. Select a product in the inventory grid.
2. Click Adjust Quantity.
3. Enter a positive number to add inventory.
4. Enter a negative number to remove inventory.
5. Click Save.

Inventory quantity cannot be reduced below zero.

RESTOCK NOTIFICATION
The "Products needing restock" message displays the number of active
products whose quantity is at or below the product's Restock Threshold.

DISCONTINUE PRODUCT
1. Select a product in the inventory grid.
2. Click Discontinue.
3. Confirm the action.

A discontinued product is not deleted from the database. It remains
available to managers for inventory history and reporting but is no
longer available for customer purchase.

RESTORE PRODUCT
A discontinued product can be restored:
1. Select the discontinued product.
2. Click Discontinue again.
3. Confirm that the product should be restored.

The product will become available for sale again.

CLOSE
Click Close to return to the Manager Dashboard.
