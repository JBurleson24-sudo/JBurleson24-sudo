MANAGER POINT OF SALE HELP

CUSTOMER

Lookup Customer:
Use this button to search for a customer before completing a sale.

Customers can be searched by information such as:
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Member / Person ID
- Order / Invoice number

After selecting a customer, the customer's name will appear on the POS screen.

Transaction History:
Displays past purchases for the currently selected customer.


PRODUCTS

Search:
Enter part of a product name and click Search.

Category:
Select a category to limit the product results.

Clear:
Clears the current product search and returns to all categories.

Product Details:
Selecting a product displays its name, description, price, image, and current stock.

Quantity:
Select the number of units to add to the cart.

Add to Cart:
Adds the selected quantity of the product to the current sale.


CART

The cart displays:
- Product name
- Price
- Quantity
- Line total

Remove Item:
Select an item in the cart and click Remove Item to remove it.

Subtotal:
Total price before discounts and tax.

Discount:
Amount removed by the currently applied promotion.

Tax:
Sales tax calculated after discounts.

Total:
Final amount charged to the customer.


PROMOTIONS

Available Promos:
Displays currently active and unexpired promotional codes.

Apply:
Enter a promotion code and click Apply.

Promotions may apply to:
- The entire order
- A specific product

Promotions may be:
- Percentage discounts
- Dollar amount discounts


CHECKOUT

A customer must be selected before checkout.

The cart must contain at least one item.

Click Checkout to enter payment information and complete the sale.

After a successful sale:
- The transaction is stored under the selected customer.
- The logged-in manager is recorded as the employee who completed the sale.
- Inventory quantities are reduced.
- An HTML sales receipt opens in the default web browser.
- The receipt includes the customer's information and the manager's name.


CLOSE

Click Close to exit the Point of Sale screen.
