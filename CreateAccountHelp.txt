CREATE ACCOUNT HELP

PERSONAL INFORMATION
--------------------
• Enter your first and last name.
• Select your title.
• Enter your address information.

LOGIN INFORMATION
-----------------
Username:
- 8-20 characters
- At least one letter
- At least one number

Password:
- 8-20 characters
- One uppercase letter
- One lowercase letter
- One number
- One special character

CONTACT INFORMATION
-------------------
Email:
- Example: user@email.com

Phone:
- 10 digits

ZIP Code:
- 5 digits

SECURITY QUESTIONS
------------------
- Choose three different questions.
- Enter an answer for each question.

When all validation messages display a green check mark,
click Create Account.
